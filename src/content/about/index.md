Here’s the honest truth about CleanServ.

We’re not a massive, impersonal franchise. We’re not a gig-economy app that sends a different stranger to your home every week.

We’re a local Johannesburg team based in Randburg, serving homes and businesses across Mzansi. We clean offices, houses, guest houses, and industrial spaces for people who want it done right — without the drama.

## How We Got Started

Like most good ideas, this one came from a simple need: reliable, high-quality cleaning without the stress.

Our founder saw that people were tired of cleaners who didn’t show up, inconsistent quality, and surprise fees. So we started CleanServ. Not to take over the world. Just to do one thing well: send trained, background-checked, reliable teams to spaces that actually need them.

No subscription traps. No “surge pricing.” Just professional cleaning. Done properly. Every time.

## What We Actually Do

**Home & Office Cleaning**

We clean while you sleep or while you work. After-hours, weekends, or during the day – whatever suits you. From daily desk wiping to deep carpet cleaning, we handle it all.

**Specialized Services**

Windows streaky? Carpets stained? Solar panels dusty? Industrial floor grimy? We have teams trained specifically for these jobs. Crystal-clear windows. Deep-cleaned carpets. Efficient solar panels. Compliant industrial spaces.

**Same-Day & Emergency Cleaning**

Life happens. Unexpected guests show up. A pipe bursts. You just can't face the mess. We accommodate urgent bookings whenever possible. Call us. We'll try to get someone to you today.

## What Makes Us Different

**You’ll know who’s coming.**

Before we send anyone to your home or business, our supervisors ensure the right team is assigned. No surprises. No “we’ll send whoever’s available.”

**We’re background-checked and trained.**

Every cleaner undergoes strict background checks, professional training, and continuous supervision. Your safety and security are non-negotiable.

**We bring our own supplies.**

Our teams arrive fully equipped with high-quality cleaning equipment and eco-friendly products. You don’t need to provide a thing.

**We’re available when you need us.**

Seven days a week, including weekends and public holidays. Early morning, late evening, or same-day if we can. We offer complete flexibility.

**We serve all of Mzansi.**

From bustling offices in Sandton to cozy homes in Ferndale, guest houses in Bromhof to industrial sites in Benrose – we’re your local cleaners with a national reach.

## Our Cleaning Packages (Simplified)

**Sparkle Package** – Dusting, vacuuming, window cleaning, trash removal, basic bathroom sanitizing. Ideal for quick maintenance and small spaces.

**Shine Package** – Everything in Sparkle, plus deep carpet and upholstery cleaning, kitchen degreasing, bathroom scrubbing, and spot treatment. Perfect for regular residential or office cleaning.

**Prestige Package** – Everything in Shine, plus industrial kitchen cleaning, high-level window cleaning, floor stripping and waxing, and a completely customized plan. Comprehensive deep cleaning for large or commercial spaces.
