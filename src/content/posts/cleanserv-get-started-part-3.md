## Managing Your Ongoing Cleaning Service

In [Part 1](/cleanserv-guide-part-1), we assessed your facility and set up your initial cleaning plan. In [Part 2](/cleanserv-guide-part-2), we explored service components and quality standards. Now, let's learn how to manage your ongoing service, scale as your business grows, and leverage advanced features for a seamless partnership.

CleanServ delivers consistent, scheduled service by default, but we also support adjustments for growth, seasonal changes, and special events. Let's explore the management process and available options.

### Activating Your Service

Before service begins, CleanServ completes the activation process:

```bash
# CleanServ Service Activation Checklist

Step 1: Finalize Service Agreement
├── Review and sign service agreement
├── Confirm start date
├── Provide facility access (keys, codes, security passes)
└── Designate client point of contact

Step 2: Team Assignment
├── Assign primary cleaning team (2-3 consistent cleaners)
├── Assign supervisor
├── Conduct site-specific training (your facility layout and priorities)
└── Provide team with access and instructions

Step 3: First Service Day
├── Team arrives at scheduled time
├── Supervisor present for first cleaning
├── Walk-through with client (if available)
└── Quality verification before team departs

Step 4: First Week Review
├── Supervisor inspection daily
├── Client feedback call (Day 3 or 4)
├── Adjustments made based on feedback
└── Confirmation that service meets expectations
