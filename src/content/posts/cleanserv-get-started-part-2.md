## Building Your Cleaning Plan with Service Components

In [Part 1](/cleanserv-guide-part-1), we covered the basics of professional cleaning and how to assess your facility's needs. Now, let's dive deeper into one of CleanServ's most powerful features: customizable service components.

Service components are the building blocks of your cleaning plan. They're discrete, measurable tasks that combine to form a complete cleaning solution for your facility. Unlike one-size-fits-all approaches, CleanServ components allow us to build exactly what you need—nothing more, nothing less.

### Anatomy of a CleanServ Service Component

Every service component has three main elements:

1. **Task Definition** - Exactly what gets cleaned and how
2. **Frequency Specification** - How often the task is performed
3. **Quality Standard** - How we measure success

## Restroom Sanitization Protocol

### Task Definition:
- Clean and disinfect all toilet surfaces (exterior, bowl, base)
- Clean and disinfect all urinal surfaces
- Clean and disinfect all sink basins, faucets, and handles
- Clean and disinfect countertops and vanities
- Clean all mirrors to streak-free finish
- Sweep and mop restroom floors
- Replenish soap, paper towels, and toilet paper
- Empty and sanitize waste bins
- Wipe down stall partitions and doors
- Check and note any maintenance issues (leaks, broken fixtures)

### Quality Standard:
- No visible dirt, dust, or residue on any surface
- No unpleasant odors
- All dispensers at least 75% full
- Mirrors streak-free
- Floors dry and slip-resistant

### Verification:
- Cleaner initials checklist in restroom log
- Supervisor spot-check weekly
- Client inspection encouraged (report any misses within 24 hours)
