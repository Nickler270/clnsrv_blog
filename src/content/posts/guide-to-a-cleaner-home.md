## Understanding Home Cleaning (It's Not About Being Perfect)

Here's something most cleaning experts won't tell you.

Your home doesn't need to look like a magazine. It needs to be functional, comfortable, and healthy. That's it.

**The goal isn't "spotless."**
**The goal is "not stressful."**

Once you accept that, cleaning becomes manageable instead of overwhelming.

## The Three Types of Home Cleaning

Not all cleaning is the same. Understanding the difference changes everything.

### Daily Cleaning (The Non-Negotiables)

This is the 10-15 minutes a day that keeps your home from becoming a disaster zone.

**What counts as daily cleaning:**
- Dishes washed or in dishwasher
- Counters wiped down
- Floors swept in high-traffic areas (kitchen, entry)
- Trash taken out if full
- Surfaces cleared of clutter

**What does NOT count as daily cleaning:**
- Scrubbing the shower
- Mopping every floor
- Cleaning inside the fridge
- Washing windows

> **The trick:** Daily cleaning is about maintenance, not deep cleaning. Lower your standards for "done" and you'll actually do it.

### Weekly Cleaning (The Reset)

This is when your home actually gets clean. Block out 1-2 hours once a week.

**What to do weekly:**
- Vacuum all carpets and rugs
- Mop hard floors
- Clean bathrooms fully (toilets, showers, sinks, mirrors)
- Dust all surfaces
- Change bed linens
- Take out recycling and trash from all rooms

**Real talk:** Weekly cleaning takes time. There's no shortcut. But doing it consistently means it never takes *more* time.

### Monthly/Seasonal Cleaning (The Deep Stuff)

This is the cleaning you forget about until suddenly it's really bad.

**Monthly tasks:**
- Wipe down baseboards
- Clean inside microwave and oven
- Wipe cabinet fronts
- Clean light fixtures and ceiling fans
- Vacuum under furniture

**Seasonal tasks (every 3-4 months):**
- Wash windows (inside and out)
- Deep clean refrigerator (empty, wipe shelves)
- Clean out closets and drawers
- Wash curtains or blinds
- Vacuum upholstery and under couch cushions

> **Pro tip:** Put these on your calendar. If you wait until you notice them, they'll already be disgusting.

## Choosing Your Cleaning Style (There's More Than One Way)

### The Daily Dasher

You do a little bit every day. Ten minutes here. Fifteen minutes there. By the end of the week, you've cleaned without ever spending hours on it.

**Best for:** People who hate marathon cleaning sessions. People with unpredictable schedules.

**How to do it:**
- Monday: bathrooms (10 min)
- Tuesday: floors (15 min)
- Wednesday: dusting (10 min)
- Thursday: kitchen deep wipe (10 min)
- Friday: trash and linens (10 min)

### The Weekend Warrior

You ignore cleaning all week, then spend Saturday morning in a cleaning frenzy.

**Best for:** People who prefer to get things done in one go. People who don't mind a little chaos during the week.

**How to do it:**
- Block 2 hours on Saturday or Sunday
- Put on music or a podcast
- Move room to room
- Don't stop until it's done

### The Outsourcer

You hire someone else to clean. (This is where CleanServ comes in.)

**Best for:** People who value their time more than the cost of cleaning. People who want it done right without thinking about it.

**How to do it:**
- Schedule regular service (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly)
- Do a 5-minute tidy before they arrive (pick up clutter)
- Let professionals handle the scrubbing

> **No judgment here.** Every option is valid. The right choice is whatever actually gets done.

## The Tools That Actually Make a Difference

You don't need a cabinet full of fancy products. You need a few things that work.

### The Starter Kit (Under R500)

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Microfiber cloths (6-10) | Dusting, wiping, glass (no streaks) |
| All-purpose cleaner | Counters, sinks, surfaces |
| Glass cleaner | Windows, mirrors, glass tables |
| Scrub brush | Bathrooms, grout, stubborn spots |
| Good vacuum | Carpets, rugs, hard floors |
| Mop and bucket | Hard floors |

### The Upgrade Kit (Worth the Money)

- **Cordless vacuum** — Grab and go. You'll vacuum more often if it's easy.
- **Squeegee** — Clear shower walls in 30 seconds. No water spots. No soap scum.
- **Grout brush** — Small, stiff, gets into cracks your regular brush misses.
- **Microfiber mop** — Washable pads. No gross bucket water sitting for weeks.

### What You Don't Need

- Ten different cleaners for ten different surfaces (all-purpose is fine)
- Single-use wipes (expensive, wasteful, less effective than cloth+spray)
- "As seen on TV" gadgets (almost all garbage)

## Room-by-Room Strategies

### Kitchen

**The problem:** Grease, crumbs, spills, and the sink that never empties.

**The strategy:**
- Clean as you cook (don't let things pile up)
- Wipe counters every night (30 seconds)
- Run dishwasher every night (empty it in the morning)
- Once a week: wipe cabinet fronts, microwave inside, sink deep clean

**The honest truth:** A clean kitchen is 80% about the sink and counters. Focus there. Everything else is bonus.

### Bathroom

**The problem:** Soap scum, hair, toothpaste spit, and the general grossness of being wet all the time.

**The strategy:**
- Keep a squeegee in the shower (30 seconds after each use)
- Keep wipes under the sink (wipe counters daily)
- Once a week: full scrub of toilet, shower, sink, mirrors

**The honest truth:** Daily maintenance makes weekly cleaning take 10 minutes instead of 30. Squeegee and wipes are non-negotiable.

### Living Areas

**The problem:** Clutter. So much clutter. Blankets, pillows, mail, remotes, kids' toys, dog toys, your phone, a half-empty coffee cup from yesterday.

**The strategy:**
- Have a basket for each room (toss clutter in, deal with it later)
- Vacuum weekly (more often with pets)
- Dust weekly (top to bottom so dust falls on already-dirty floors)

**The honest truth:** Clutter makes a clean room look messy. Pick up first. Then clean. Order matters.

### Bedrooms

**The problem:** Laundry piles, nightstand clutter, and sheets that haven't been changed in... let's not think about it.

**The strategy:**
- Make the bed every morning (60 seconds, changes everything)
- Have a laundry basket (not the floor)
- Change sheets weekly (buy two sets so you can swap without waiting for laundry)

**The honest truth:** A made bed makes the whole room feel 50% cleaner. Even if the floor is a disaster.

## The Real Secrets (What Actually Works)

**Secret 1: Done is better than perfect.**

Your baseboards don't need to be bleached. Your windows don't need to be streak-free. Clean enough is clean enough.

**Secret 2: Five minutes is better than zero minutes.**

Don't have time to clean the whole bathroom? Clean the toilet. That's it. It's still progress.

**Secret 3: Everything has a home.**

If things don't have a place, they'll end up on your counters, tables, and floors. Every item needs a spot. Even if that spot is "the junk drawer."

**Secret 4: You can't organize your way out of too much stuff.**

Less stuff = less cleaning. If you haven't used it in a year, get rid of it. The clutter isn't sentimental. It's just dust waiting to happen.

## When to DIY vs When to Hire Help

**DIY if:**
- You have the time and energy
- You actually enjoy cleaning (some people do, weirdly)
- Your home is small or low-traffic
- You're on a tight budget

**Hire help if:**
- You have the money but not the time
- Cleaning causes arguments in your household
- You have health issues that make cleaning difficult
- You just hate it and can afford not to do it

> **Zero shame in hiring help.** You hire a mechanic for your car. An electrician for your wiring. A cleaner for your home. Same thing.

It's about doing something. Consistently. Even when you don't want to.

Ten minutes a day. One hour on the weekend. Whatever you can manage.

Your home doesn't need to be perfect. It just needs to be a place you don't dread coming home to.
